Why Sell to Rajharita Naturals?

We buy crops directly from farmers — no middlemen, no commission, no delayed payments. When you sell to us, you get a fair price for your hard work and a partner who respects your time. Our team visits your village for pickup, and payment reaches your account within 48 hours of collection. Every season, we buy — so you always have a reliable buyer for your crop.

How It Works

Getting started is easy and takes only a few minutes.
Step 1: Fill the short form below with your name, village, crop type and quantity available.
Step 2: Our team calls you within 24 hours to discuss price and pickup details.
Step 3: Once you agree, we visit your village, collect the crop, and weigh it in front of you.
Step 4: Payment is transferred directly to your bank account or UPI within 48 hours. That's it — simple, transparent and fair.
