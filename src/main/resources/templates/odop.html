Partner With Us — Grow Your District

Work with Rajharita Naturals on your district's ODOP product

Why Partner With Rajharita Naturals?

Every district in India has a story — a signature product that carries its identity, culture and craft. Under the ODOP (One District One Product) initiative, we work with local partners who want to take that product to the rest of India. Whether you own the land, run the operations, bring investment, or connect us with farmers — we want to work with you. Together, we can build a brand that respects tradition and rewards everyone in the chain.

Partnership Roles We Offer

We structure every ODOP partnership based on what you bring to the table.
Land Owner: contribute land for cultivation or processing in exchange for a fixed lease or profit share.
Operations Partner: manage day-to-day production, logistics or processing locally.
Investment Partner: fund a specific district's ODOP line and share in its revenues.
Farmer Network Partner: connect us with local kisan families so we can source directly. Whichever role fits you, we finalize clear terms with full transparency.

How It Works
